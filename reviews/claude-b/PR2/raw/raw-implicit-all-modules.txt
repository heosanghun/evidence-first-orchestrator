########## D. what this does NOT cover, stated rather than implied ##########

  `x.foo` is only unsafe if x can be None or another type, and
  deciding that needs type inference this probe does not have. Listing
  every attribute access would be a census of ~thousands of sites with
  no adjudication behind it - the appearance of coverage, not coverage.
  For scale: 963 attribute accesses in these modules.

    MEASURED - every constant-key subscript read in the package is
               adjudicated; the 3 integer indexes are guarded by
               short-circuit ordering; no module outside workspace.py
               reads from a repair-rebuilt projection.

               StopIteration shapes anywhere, and dynamic-key
               subscripts (x[variable]) everywhere.
